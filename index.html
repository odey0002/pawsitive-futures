Luna’s story

Luna found her quiet place.

Luna arrived at Pawsitive Futures unsure of new people and happiest watching from a safe corner. After several weeks in foster care, she was matched with Maya, whose quiet home gave her the space to settle in at her own pace.

Four months later, Luna has claimed the sunny window as her favourite spot, follows Maya from room to room, and is now the first to ask for attention at the end of the day.

“She started out hiding under the sofa. Now she follows me everywhere and sleeps beside me every night.”
