Volunteer with us

Your time can become someone’s second chance.

You don’t need professional animal-care experience to make a difference. We match volunteers with roles based on their interests, skills, comfort level, and availability.

Animal care

Help pets feel safe and settled.

Assist with feeding, enrichment, cleaning, and everyday routines that keep animals comfortable.

Time commitment
2–3 hour shifts, at least once a week.
Training
Orientation and basic animal-care training before your first shift.
Events & adoption days

Help people meet their match.

Welcome visitors, prepare spaces, support meet-and-greets, and help community events run smoothly.

Time commitment
4–6 hour shifts on scheduled event days.
Training
A short event briefing and on-site guidance are provided.
Transport

Help animals get where they need to go.

Support safe transportation to foster homes, veterinary appointments, and adoption events.

Time commitment
1–3 hours per trip, based on scheduled transport needs.
Training
Transport and safety guidance are provided before your first trip.
Creative & community

Use the skills you already have.

Contribute photography, social content, administration, storytelling, or community outreach.

Time commitment
2–4 hours per week or per project, depending on the task.
Training
A project brief and guidance are provided before you begin.

  1. 01

Is volunteering right for you?

What we look for.

  • Comfort following animal-handling and safety guidance.
  • Reliable and respectful of scheduled commitments.
  • Willing to attend a short orientation before starting.
  • Some roles may have minimum-age or supervision requirements.
  • Animal-handling experience is helpful, but not required for every role.

Foster a pet

A temporary home can change everything.

Foster families give animals somewhere safe to rest, recover, and build confidence while we search for the permanent home that suits them best.

I’m interested in fostering →

How long will I foster?

Placements vary depending on the animal and situation. We’ll discuss the expected commitment before a placement begins.

What do I need to provide?

A safe, suitable environment, patience, basic daily care, and regular updates while the animal is with you.

Will I have support?

Yes. Pawsitive Futures stays part of the process and provides guidance throughout the foster placement.
